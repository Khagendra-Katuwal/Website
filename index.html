Hello Everyone

My name is Khagendra Katuwal, and I was born and raised in the eastern part of the beautiful country of Nepal. Growing up, I always dreamed of becoming a medical doctor. However, life had a different plan for me, and somehow, my journey led me from dreaming about medicine to exploring the mysteries of the universe. Today, instead of studying the human body, I study our closest star the Sun!

I completed most of my education in Nepal, including my Master's degree in Physics from the Central Department of Physics at Tribhuvan University in Kathmandu. My growing curiosity about the universe and passion for astronomy inspired me to pursue higher education in the United States. That dream eventually brought me to New Mexico State University (NMSU)strong>, where I am currently in the final year of my Ph.D. in Astronomy and Astrophysics.

My research focuses on understanding the solar atmosphere, magnetic activity, and space weather. I am particularly interested in how the Sun's magnetic field influences its atmosphere and how solar activity affects the space environment around Earth. Although research is an important part of my academic journey, teaching has always held a special place in my heart. I have been involved in education for many years, and I genuinely enjoy sharing knowledge, working with students, and helping others develop an interest in science. Beyond research and teaching, I strongly believe that science should not remain confined to universities, research laboratories, or scientific publications. It should reach everyone. With this belief, I have been actively involved in science education and public outreach since 2018, organizing and participating in numerous national and international activities to promote astronomy and scientific curiosity.

Over the years, I have developed experience in designing scientific investigations, analyzing observational data, writing research manuscripts, teaching, mentoring students, and organizing science outreach programs. Looking back, my journey from a small part of eastern Nepal to studying the Sun in the United States has been full of unexpected turns, challenges, and opportunities.

I may not have become the medical doctor I once dreamed of being, but I discovered a different passion—one that allows me to explore the universe, contribute to scientific knowledge, and inspire others to become curious about the world around them.

"And perhaps that is the most beautiful part of my journey: sometimes life takes us somewhere we never planned, only to help us discover what we truly love."
